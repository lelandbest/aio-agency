## Missing Capabilities

1. **No logo upload/display** - companyLogo field not used
2. **No branding persistence** - changes don't save to backend
3. **No brand config object** - scattered across components
4. **No report letterhead** - Cortex reports have no branding
5. **No tenant-aware branding** - branding is app-wide, not per-tenant
6. **No footer/disclaimer fields** - can't customize report footers
7. **No contact info fields** - can't add contact info to reports
