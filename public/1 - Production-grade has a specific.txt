5 - Error handling is the skill no one teaches. When your AI workflow hits an unexpected input, most prompts produce something off-target and keep going. Production-grade prompts include explicit instructions for what to do when conditions aren't met. "If you cannot find X, say so and stop" is not a hack. It's basic workflow hygiene.
