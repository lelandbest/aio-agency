Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about AIO CRM.

What AI models does AIO CRM support?

AIO CRM connects to Ollama (local), OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google AI, and Perplexity. You can use any combination and switch between providers based on your needs.

Is my data private?

100%. With Ollama, all AI processing happens on your infrastructure. Your CRM data never touches third-party servers unless you choose cloud AI providers.

How does Signal Orchestration work?

Signals detect patterns and changes in your CRM data. Orchestration executes risk-aware actions with configurable confirmation workflows, full audit logging, and escalation hooks.

Can I customize the AI guardrails?

Yes. Professional and Enterprise plans include custom system and task-level guardrails. Define behavior constraints, tone guidelines, and output requirements for your AI.

What does white-label include?

Enterprise white-label covers custom logos, color schemes, report letterhead, and domain deployment. Your clients see your brand, not ours.

How do I get started?

Start with the free trial. Connect your AI provider, import contacts, and the Neural Cortex starts learning your business immediately. No credit card required.
