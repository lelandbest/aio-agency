AIO Nexus Operator's Guide

The Zero-Cloud-Rent Local Neural Appliance for Solo Operators (Podcasters, Tech Directors, Video Editors & Designers)

Philosophy: Zero Cloud Rent & Total Data Sovereignty

AIO Nexus operates entirely on your local hardware. There are no monthly SaaS user seats, no per-token external AI billing, and no remote database dependencies. All AI reasoning is handled via local Ollama models, all video is rendered locally with Remotion, and speech is transcribed locally with Vosk/Whisper.

1. System Quickstart & Daily Boot

To launch the appliance, run the 1-click startup script from your terminal or double-click:

cd d:\AIOCRM
start-nexus.bat

The launcher performs system pre-flight checks, verifies local Ollama status, boots the FastAPI backend (:8001), verifies health, starts the Vite React frontend (:3000), and automatically opens your browser.

Surface URL Access Role
Desktop Cockpit http://localhost:3000 Full studio control center with deep canvas tooling
Mobile / Pocket http://localhost:3000?view=pocket Touch-optimized mobile cockpit for phone/tablet
Backend REST API http://localhost:8001/api/health System telemetry and diagnostics

2. Workstation Modules

🧠 Cortex (Brain)

Knowledge vault with dense local vector embeddings (nomic-embed-text). Ingest transcripts, briefs, and notes with instant semantic retrieval.

🎬 Media Studio

Local Remotion video renderer, waveform trimmer, and BOOM recording studio for high-resolution video snippets and audiograms.

⚡ Flows (Automation)

Visual canvas for building multi-step execution graphs linking triggers, specialist agents, and output channels.

📱 Pocket Cockpit

Standalone mobile portal for on-set directors: 1-tap approval clearances, Charlie voice directives, and live cue sheets.

3. Turnkey SOB Blueprints

Blueprint A: Solo Podcaster & Creative Studio (podcast_creator)

  • Guest Intake: Custom form captures headshot, bio, and discussion topics.
  • Local Speech-to-Text: Offline transcription generates synchronized subtitles.
  • Content Extraction: Echo extracts soundbites; Archer writes show notes and timestamps.
  • Video Generation: Remotion renders vertical audiogram clips.
  • Distribution: PostBot queues 5 social media announcements ready for 1-tap clearance.

Blueprint B: Live Event Technical Director (tech_director)

  • Run-of-Show Synthesis: Atlas compiles production rider into a synchronized cue timeline.
  • On-Set Pocket Viewer: High-contrast OLED live display with segment duration timers.
  • GO Trigger: Instant advance button to signal next cue to stage crew.
  • Contingency Loops: 1-click trigger to stage emergency video loop if presenter goes overtime.

4. Mobile "Pocket Cockpit" (Away From Desk)

When you leave your main studio desk, open http://<your-ip>:3000?view=pocket on your phone or tablet:

  • Approvals Queue: Clear paused AI execution runs with a swipe or tap.
  • Charlie Voice Directives: Push-to-talk voice interface for dictating commands while walking or driving.
  • Run-of-Show Cues: High-contrast live cue sheet with live countdown timer.
  • Quick Vault Capture: Instantly snap photos, record voice memos, or jot notes straight into Cortex.

5. Remote Phone Access (Tailscale WireGuard)

To securely access your studio appliance from anywhere in the world without exposing router ports:

  1. Install Tailscale on your studio PC/Mac and on your phone.
  2. Sign into the same account on both devices.
  3. On your phone, open Safari or Chrome to: http://<studio-tailscale-ip>:3000?view=pocket.
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen to install the standalone mobile app.

6. Backups & Data Sovereignty

Your data lives exclusively in backend/data/. To backup your entire business appliance:

xcopy /E /I /H /Y d:\AIOCRM\backend\data d:\Backups\AIOCRM-data

To restore on any PC or Mac, paste the folder and run start-nexus.bat.
